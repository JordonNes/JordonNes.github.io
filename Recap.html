SIGNIFICANT POSITIVE RESULTSSURPRISES / MODEL WINS

Call out the previous day's results that materially outperformed expectations or confirmed an important L&J read: unusually strong player ceilings, unexpected role growth, dominant matchup channels, correctly identified under/over pivots, successful upset calls, strong tennis surface or serve-return reads, and any lower-confidence prediction that proved materially better than expected.

SIGNIFICANT NEGATIVE RESULTSSURPRISES / MODEL FAILURES

Call out high-confidence misses and materially unexpected failures: workload reductions, late scratches, blowout effects, injury, foul trouble, bullpen collapse, pace changes, quarterback or lineup changes, tennis retirement/serve breakdowns, fighter game-plan surprises, bad market selection, or a statistical channel that L&J clearly misread.

🎾 TENNIS RECAP STANDARDATP • WTA • GRAND SLAMS

Tennis predictions are graded exactly like the other sports. Match winners, game handicaps, total games, set markets and verified player statistics are audited after matches are final. Retirements, walkovers, defaults and market-specific settlement issues are classified as HIT, MISS, PUSH/VOID or UNGRADED according to the exact published market rather than forced into a result.

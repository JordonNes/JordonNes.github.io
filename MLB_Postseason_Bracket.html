L&J RESEARCH BOARD

What moved the bracket beyond seed order

HOU over CWS — narrow exception to season record

Chicago owns the better record and run differential, but Houston won five of seven head-to-head games, hosts the entire best-of-three, and enters with Yordan Alvarez back in elite form. The Astros' thin rotation is why this stays a 55% lean rather than a strong pick.

EDGE: Alvarez • home field • 5–2 season series
RISK: Houston finished 24th in team ERA / weakest playoff pitching profile.

NYY over BOS — pitching wins the tiebreak

New York's 3.24 team ERA was MLB's best and the rotation has multiple playoff-ace options. Boston's staff is also strong, and Aaron Judge's calf status materially lowers L&J confidence. New York won the season series 7–6 and went 4–2 against Boston in the Bronx.

EDGE: rotation depth • home field • run differential
RISK: Judge availability / Boston's second-half improvement.

ATL over PHI — strongest Wild Card mismatch signal

Atlanta went 9–4 against Philadelphia. Chris Sale enters with a 2.16 ERA and has historically controlled Bryce Harper and Kyle Schwarber. Philadelphia reaches the round with Zack Wheeler off Game 162, Jesús Luzardo returning from a shoulder issue and a bullpen that posted a 5.79 ERA since Sept. 1.

EDGE: Sale • rest alignment • matchup history
RISK: Philadelphia's October experience and top-end rotation talent.

SD over CHC — JINX context beats raw season matchup

Chicago's offense is the best in this field and it went 5–1 against San Diego, so LEGZ does not like fading the Cubs. JINX moves the series narrowly to San Diego because the Padres finished hot, play all games at Petco, and can protect late leads with Adrian Morejon and Mason Miller.

EDGE: bullpen • late form • home series
RISK: Cubs offense and dominant 2026 head-to-head results.

MIL is the bracket's strongest season-long profile

The Brewers finished 103–59 with a +214 run differential, ranked near the top in both run creation and pitching, and ended 8–2 with five straight wins. Their main October concern is rotation depth behind Jacob Misiorowski and Logan Henderson.

EDGE: balance • run prevention • first-round bye
RISK: back-half rotation depth in a long series.

LAD gets the championship nod on ceiling

Milwaukee owns the best regular-season résumé, but Los Angeles pairs a top-two playoff offense with a top-three pitching staff and a potentially dominant four-man October rotation. Current championship markets also make the Dodgers the shortest-priced team, which L&J uses only as an external sanity check.

EDGE: rotation ceiling • lineup depth • multiple elite stars
RISK: bullpen volatility and health of key stars.

What the rule tests
  1. At minute 5, check whether a side’s midpoint dipped at least 3¢ from minute 3 to 4 and rebounded at least 1¢ by minute 5, with a spread no wider than 4¢.
  2. Model entry at minute 6’s ask, restricted to 10–80¢. Both UP and DOWN use the same rule.
  3. Observe whether a later bid reaches 8¢ above entry by minute 11. Cap a target-exit scenario at that target; otherwise use minute 11’s bid.
  4. Compare that exit with selling at minute 11 on the same entries. Fees and slippage are assumptions applied to hypothetical full fills.

The study excludes incomplete required quote paths. Reported rates describe that covered subset; missing paths may bias it. Candle closes cannot establish intraminute order, queue priority, depth, partial fills or auto-sell execution.

What must be verified before a recommendation
  • Forward evidence, depth, fill behavior, applicable fees and slippage.

Current fee metadata does not verify historical or account-specific charges. The cost illustration assumes taker fees on both legs and ten hypothetical contracts. A resting auto-sell can have different fees and execution behavior. This study does not model a downside stop or prove settlement forecasting skill.

Position discipline

Damilola — Prefilter: determine whether the contract belongs in the approved category, whether settlement terms and quotes are clear, and whether historical price behavior supports a directional thesis.

Gavavi — Execution: evaluate YES and NO asks, liquidity, timing, historical opportunity and fees. Recommend YES or NO only when a tested model estimates a positive risk-adjusted edge; otherwise WAIT or SKIP.

A cheap contract is not automatically profitable. A high market-implied probability is not independent model confidence. Fee, spread, settlement and loss risks apply to every contract. Quotes are indicative until verified at execution time.

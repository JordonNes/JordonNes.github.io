What D&G is being built to answer
  1. Starting position: underlying price above/below target, opening momentum, source and time.
  2. Settlement outlook: independently validated probability of ending above/below target, with uncertainty.
  3. Supporting evidence: comparable prior windows, sample size, calibration and out-of-sample results.
  4. Early-exit outlook: probability and timing of a favorable contract-price move, adverse excursion, executable depth and net economics.

An auto-sell is a resting limit sell on Kalshi. A favorable underlying reversal does not guarantee your contract-price target or a complete fill. No orders are placed by this page.

D&G v 0.1 remains the saved baseline. Pattern Lab is an exploratory extension. All times are labeled Pacific; raw timestamps are UTC. No trading enabled.

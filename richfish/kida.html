CLEAN-CATCH SHEET · STRIPED BASS

Whole striper → clean fillets

Scale or skin?
Keeping skin: scale tail to head. Removing skin: scaling is optional.
Head cut.
Cut immediately behind the gill plate/pectoral-fin area toward the head, stopping at the backbone.
Find the spine.
Open the dorsal line with short controlled strokes and let the backbone guide the blade.
Open the fillet.
Lift the meat as the blade follows the skeleton toward the tail.
Follow the ribs.
Work over and around the rib cage instead of blindly chopping through it.
Release and repeat.
Free the first fillet, flip the fish, and repeat on side two.
Skin if desired.
Start at the tail with skin down; grip the skin and keep the blade nearly flat.
Finish.
Feel for bones, remove remaining rib/pin bones, trim unwanted dark bloodline/red meat and excess belly material, pat dry and chill promptly.

Kida's Clean-Catch Guide · Striped Bass

1 · Scale?

Scale tail-to-head if keeping skin. Skin-off fillets do not require scaling.

2 · Gill Plate

Cut behind the gill plate toward the head, stopping at the backbone.

3 · Follow Spine

Use short strokes and keep the blade against the skeleton.

4 · Rib Cage

Follow over the ribs instead of chopping blindly through them.

5 · Release

Free the fillet and repeat on side two.

6 · Skin

Skin-side down, grip the tail and keep the blade nearly flat.

7 · Finish

Feel for bones and trim unwanted dark bloodline/red meat.

8 · Store

Pat dry, chill promptly and package for refrigerator or freezer.

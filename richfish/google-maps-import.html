Import saved Google Maps locations

This tool imports Google Takeout Saved CSV collections directly in your browser. The CSV contents are not uploaded to RICHFISH, GitHub, or a server. They stay in this browser's local storage until you clear them. This is a manual import, not continuous Google Maps synchronization.

1. Select CSV files

Select the normalized RICHFISH private JSON inventory, or extract your Google Takeout ZIP and choose Nesbitt_s Fishing Spots.csv and boat launch.csv.

2. Review your saved places

No collections imported yet.

3. Verify coordinates and promote to FRmap

Google Takeout may supply a Maps place URL without coordinates. These records remain unverified map positions until latitude/longitude is independently confirmed. Do not infer shore access, legal fishing, species availability, or safe consumption from a saved place alone. Use a saved location’s “Set coordinates” action, paste a latitude/longitude from the exact fishing access point, then explicitly approve it for your private RICHFISH working map. This does not certify legal public access or fishing safety. You can refine the site in Location Manager.

Verify location

Copy exact coordinates from Google Maps for the fishing access point, not the center of the surrounding city or bay. Coordinates must be reviewed manually.

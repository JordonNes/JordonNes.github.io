THE L&J EVALUATION PIPELINE

Probability, value and economics stay separate
Season Schedule→ Roster / Player Registry→ Exact POM Feed→ LEGZ statistical spectrum→ JINX current-context review→ LJPC→ Hot Top / 20 Piece / QC→ Settlement & Calibration

LSI prepares the schedule, player identity and historical profile before the market arrives. When an exact POM is acquired, LEGZ evaluates it from stored evidence, JINX checks what changed, and only then may the POM receive LJPC. L&J separates prediction quality from payout.

PREDICTION READINESS STANDARD

Prepare first, evaluate immediately when the market appears

Schedule & roster preload

LSI stores season or rolling schedules and persistent player identities before game day. NFL now uses a full-season schedule and roster preload; CFB uses the season schedule plus persistent player history and available depth/role context.

POM acquisition

Exact props, odds and moneylines are collected from configured market sources, including sportsbook, DFS/pick’em and prediction-market feeds when those sources are actually accessible. Source and timestamp stay attached to the POM.

Same-cycle LJPC evaluation

An acquired POM is matched to the event, player, market, threshold and side. LEGZ uses persistent history and cached distributions; JINX checks current injuries, role, weather, lineup and other material changes. The objective is same-cycle individualized LJPC rather than repeated basic research.

No fabricated completeness

If the evidence needed for a specific market is unavailable, the POM remains explicitly awaiting evidence. Market probability or consensus is never relabeled as LJPC merely to fill a board.

HOW L&J READS A PICK

Simple version

1. LEGZ studies the numbers

LEGZ reviews statistics, trends, matchup history, recent performance, market information and other useful evidence. Stronger and more consistent evidence creates a stronger LEGZ Value.

2. JINX checks the situation

JINX looks for things that can change the outcome: injuries, lineup changes, coaching decisions, role changes, travel, weather, unusual market movement and other relevant context.

3. LJPC gives the final probability

LJPC is L&J’s final estimated chance that the exact pick will hit. Higher LJPC means L&J believes the pick is more likely to succeed.

4. POM Value helps rank the best options

POM Value helps compare picks by combining how strong the evidence is with how likely L&J believes the pick is to hit. It is used to identify the most attractive prediction opportunities.

QC TICKET MODES

Exact offered POMs only

SNS1 / Goblin 1

Probability-first. Uses Goblin POMs and targets the highest LJPC legs. It is intended to be L&J’s most secure construction, subject to current market availability and provenance.

SNS2 / Goblin 2

Probability-first with diversity. Uses Goblin and/or Normal POMs and avoids merely duplicating SNS1 when alternatives clear the gate.

Normal / Market

Uses standard unmarked market POMs. Ranking is prediction-first: LJPC and POM Value dominate; market economics and source depth are tie-breakers.

Aggressive / Demon

Economics-first only after the probability gate. Qualified Normal/Demon POMs compete on payout/upside while retaining an explicit LJPC.

PROVENANCE & SETTLEMENT RULES

No reconstructed certainty
Every published pick should have a clear reason behind it. L&J does not treat guesses, missing markets or old completed events as fresh predictions. After games finish, results are graded and used to evaluate how well the system performed.
